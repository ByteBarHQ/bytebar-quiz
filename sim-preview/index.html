How our questions are built

Built like the real exam. Because we study the real exam.

Every ByteBar question goes through a four-step process before it reaches you. No trivia, no filler — just the concepts the vendors actually test.

1
Mapped to official objectives

Each question is tagged to a domain from the vendor's published exam objectives — CompTIA, AWS, Microsoft, Google, CNCF, HashiCorp, ITIL.

2
Written from vendor documentation

Scenarios are built from official docs and real-world configurations, not recycled from other quiz sites.

3
Every answer explained

Correct answers explain why. Wrong options explain the misconception they test — so a miss becomes a lesson.

4
Reviewed for exam realism

Distractors mirror the traps real exams use: the almost-right answer, the outdated best practice, the plausible misconfiguration.
